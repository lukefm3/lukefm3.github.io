Methodology & assumptions

The pipeline retrieves hourly temperature, cloud cover, and shortwave solar radiation for Honolulu, Kahului, Hilo, and Līhuʻe. It then applies a simple performance ratio to translate incoming radiation into an illustrative PV-output series.

Modeled hourly kWh per installed kWshortwave radiation (W/m²) ÷ 1,000 × 0.80

This is a portfolio demonstration—not measured production, a financial forecast, or an engineering estimate. It does not model panel tilt, shading, equipment-specific temperature effects, inverter clipping, degradation, or local interconnection constraints.
